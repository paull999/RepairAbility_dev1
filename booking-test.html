repairability – booking API test harness

Create a job, ask for its booking options (get-bookings), then confirm one (confirm-booking). Also see the create-job harness.

Connection

This works on real data: it creates a job and books it for a real servicer in the database behind the URL. Test jobs are titled TEST booking ….

1Generate a new job

Uses the create-job API. The customer needs an agreed price for the job type, and servicers who cover the postcode and have the skills, or there will be nothing to book.

2Get bookings

Returns the servicers who can do the job and a list of booking options, as the Book button on the job shows. Every option has its own key.

3Confirm booking

Click an option above (or paste any key below), then confirm. The job becomes Booked with that servicer, date and booking window, exactly as if booked in the app.
